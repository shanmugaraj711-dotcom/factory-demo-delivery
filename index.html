03Story

I don't just make websites. I build systems, products and experiments — and the machinery that keeps them getting better.

From ideas
to impact.

  1. Idea
  2. System
  3. Product
  4. Next

  1. Chapter 01 — Idea

    Every useful product starts with a problem worth solving.

    Not a trend, not a template. A real friction someone feels — and a rough thought about how it could be different.

  2. Chapter 02 — System

    Before the product, the machinery.

    Project Factory exists so that good ideas don't depend on luck: a repeatable path from idea to spec to validated, deployed software.

  3. Chapter 03 — Product

    Then, tools people actually use.

    PromptStudio AI puts creative AI to work. Abacus Buddy AI brings AI into learning through play. WhatsApp automation and client workflows are live. CartLift and APK Forge are on the bench, being built properly.

  4. Chapter 04 — Next

    Project Atlas is the long bet.

    Modular foundations for the next generation of AI products. It is unfinished — deliberately. Some things are worth taking the time to get right.
